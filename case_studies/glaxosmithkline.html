GlaxoSmithKline (GSK)

A Pharmaceutical Transnational: Profits vs Ethics

1. Company Profile
£34bnTurnover (2019)
100kEmployees
£4.6bnR&D Spend/Year
  • HQ: United Kingdom.
  • Reach: 84 manufacturing sites in 36 countries.
  • Products: Medicines (Amoxicillin / Augmentin for bacterial infections, Zidovudine for HIV, Bendazole for worms), Vaccines, Consumer Health.
  • R&D: One of the few companies researching WHO priority diseases (HIV, Malaria, TB). Research centre in Spain focuses on TB and Malaria.
2. Common Criticisms of Big Pharma
  • Profits: Excess profits above average for corporations.
  • Pricing: Overcharging for lifesaving drugs.
  • Lobbying: Spending huge amounts to influence regulations in their favour.
  • Focus: Prioritising drugs for chronic conditions (rich markets) over "forgotten" tropical diseases (poor markets) or antibiotics.
3. GSK's Ethical Policy
GSK has adopted an ethical policy towards the developing world to address the disparity in drug access.
  • Small Return: Commitment to a capped small return (5%) on products sold.
  • Tiered Pricing: Capping patented drug prices in developing countries to 25% of the UK price.
  • Access: Providing HIV/AIDS drugs to LICs at significant discounts.
  • Re-investment: Investing 20% of profits from sales in developing countries back into healthcare infrastructure in those countries.
  • Innovation: Close to launching the first effective malaria vaccine.
