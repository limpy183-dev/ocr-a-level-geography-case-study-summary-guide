Place Profiles

Jembatan Besi, Jakarta

Life in one of Jakarta's most densely populated slums

1. Location and Context
4kmNW of City Centre
60kPop Density (per km²)
$4Avg Daily Income
  • Location: A slum about 4km northwest of Jakarta city centre, hemmed in by built-up areas and the Ciliwung River.
  • Population: Over 31,000 people in just 0.5km². One of the most densely populated districts in Jakarta.
  • Inequality: In Indonesia, the wealthiest 10% control nearly 30% of wealth, while the poorest 10% have just 2.9%.
2. Social & Economic Conditions
Employment & Income
  • Income: Average ~$4/day. Employment is insecure (unskilled/casual labour).
  • Industry: Many small-scale garment producers operate in the slum with little health and safety protection.
  • Self-Employment: Selling food or second-hand goods (salvaged from waste) is common.
Health & Sanitation
  • Sanitation: Hardly exists. Few toilets; those that exist are run for profit and flush into open sewers.
  • Water: No clean running water. Groundwater is polluted (built on former waste tip).
  • Disease: Epidemics of water-borne diseases (Cholera, Typhoid). Malaria and Hepatitis A are issues.
  • Risks: Dehydration causes death in young/elderly due to diarrhoea. Cooking with kerosene risks air pollution.
  • Nutrition: Rice dominated; little fresh protein/fruit.
3. Housing Conditions
  • Construction: Ground floors are timber/brick. Extra storeys are makeshift (scrap wood/metal) as pressure on space grows.
  • Fire Risk: Constant risk due to overcrowding, use of kerosene, and makeshift electrical wiring (serious overloading).
  • Light: Narrow alleys and tall buildings block sunlight. Lighting is by neon tubes and bare light bulbs.
4. The Future
  • Slum Clearance: Clearing slums often just moves people to other slums (e.g., Ciliwung River), making things worse there.
  • Improvement: Jakarta Housing Agency has identified 392 "community units" for improvement.
  • Resilience: Strong sense of community exists, but inequalities with the rest of Jakarta remain stark.
