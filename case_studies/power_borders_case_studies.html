Choose a Case Study

Yemen

Sovereignty challenged by Houthis, Saudi-led intervention, southern separatism, terrorism and humanitarian crisis.

South Sudan

New sovereign state affected by civil war, ethnic rivalry, poor governance, displacement and multi-scale intervention.

Mali

Territorial integrity challenged by Tuareg separatism, Azawad claims, extremist activity and porous borders.

Basque Country

A cross-border nation in Spain and France, with cultural identity, autonomy and independence demands.

Kashmir

Contested territory where India and Pakistan dispute sovereignty, security and access to Indus Basin water resources.

East Timor

Successful independence after Portuguese rule, Indonesian annexation, conflict and later maritime boundary settlement.

Kurdistan

A stateless nation spread across several sovereign states, useful for explaining nation, state and nation-state.
How to Use These Pages
  • Yemen, South Sudan and Mali are the strongest extended examples because they contain detailed causes, impacts and global governance responses.
  • Basque Country, Kashmir, East Timor and Kurdistan are shorter located examples that help you compare different types of challenge.
  • Exam rule: always name whether the example challenges sovereignty, territorial integrity, or both.
