Haiti Cholera Epidemic (2010)

The British Red Cross Response & Critical Evaluation

1. Context: The 2010 Earthquake
7.0Magnitude
30-300kDeaths (Est.)
60%Pop on <$2.5 /day
  • Disaster: Devastating earthquake left hundreds of thousands homeless in makeshift camps (e.g., La Piste, Port-a-Piment).
  • Sanitation: In Port-au-Prince slums, 50% had no toilets.
  • Outbreak: Cholera began in Oct 2010, introduced by Nepalese UN soldiers. Spread rapidly.
  • Toll: By Nov 2014: 720,000 cases, 8,700 deaths.
2. British Red Cross Response (2010–12)
  • Clean Water: Delivered to 300,000 people in camps (e.g., in Port-a-Piment).
  • Hygiene: Built 1,300 latrines serving 250,000 people.
  • Medical: Provided supplies to Saint Marc hospital; treated 18,700 cases in treatment units (CTUs).
  • Awareness: Raised awareness on infection avoidance and symptoms.
3. Critical Evaluation
Criticism: Despite 12,000 NGOs present ("Republic of NGOs"), cholera killed thousands in the first few months.
  • Failures: Aid agencies accepted funds for water/sanitation but many camps (e.g., Cité Soleil) still lacked chlorinated water 10 days into the outbreak.
  • Waste: 6 months later, only one waste management site was operational for a city of 3 million.
  • Comparison: MSF (Doctors Without Borders) and Cuban doctors treated >75,000 cases while other agencies were criticized for slow implementation of control measures.
