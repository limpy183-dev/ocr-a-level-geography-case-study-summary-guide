The Rosy Periwinkle

Medicinal Plants, Biopiracy, and Conservation

1. The Plant & Medicinal Value
Native to Madagascar. Contains 70 alkaloids. Two (vincristine & vinblastine) are powerful cancer drugs.
  • Vincristine: Treats childhood leukaemia. Increased survival from 10% (1970) to >90% today.
  • Vinblastine: Treats Hodgkin’s lymphoma.
  • Economic: Global sales worth hundreds of millions of dollars annually to Eli Lilly (US Giant).
2. Biopiracy
Biopiracy: The exploitation of biological resources without fair compensation to indigenous people.
  • Inequality: Despite the huge profits for pharmaceutical companies, few benefits channel back to Madagascar or its indigenous people. Biopiracy deprives LIDCs of value-added exports.
  • Impact: Deprives LIDCs of international trade/exports, hindering economic growth and poverty reduction.
3. Conservation Issues
Over-harvesting
Most medicinal plants are wild-sourced (80% of settlers in developing world rely on them). Over-harvesting threatens survival (e.g., Yew (Taxol), Goldenseal, Nardostachys jatamansi). 4,000 species threatened. CITES protects 5000 animal and 29000 plant species.
Habitat Destruction
  • Deforestation: Tropical rainforests contain 70% of terrestrial plant species, yet <1% have been screened for medical use.
  • Loss: At least one potential major drug is lost every 2 years due to deforestation.
Sustainable Solutions (Samoa Example)
  • Prostialin: HIV drug from Mamala tree in Samoa.
  • Agreement: Profits from the drug are returned to Samoa to fund forest protection and economic development.
  • Result: Indigenous people benefit, forests are conserved, and sustainable supply is assured.
