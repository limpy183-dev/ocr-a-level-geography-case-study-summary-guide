Northwood, Irvine

A Planned Community in Southern California

1. Location and Physical Context
22kMedian Income $113k
Planned"Best Place to Live"
Edge CitySoCal Location
  • Location: Southern edge of Los Angeles conurbation, Orange County. 10km east of Newport Beach.
  • Type: A "village" within the fully planned city of Irvine (classic edge city developed on former ranch land).
  • Vision: "To create and maintain a community where people can live, work, and play in an environment that is safe, vibrant, and aesthetically pleasing."
2. Social & Economic Conditions
Wealth & Employment
  • Income: High. Median $113,603 (vs National $63,179).
  • Major Employer: The University of California, Irvine is the city's single largest employer.
  • High-Tech: Home to Blizzard Entertainment (video games), Broadcom (semiconductors).
  • TNCs: USA HQs for Kia Motors, Mazda, Toshiba.
Education & Safety
  • Education: Schools are among the best-achieving in the USA. 68.5% of residents (25+) have degrees; 20.5% have masters/doctorates.
  • Crime: Violent crime is ~50 incidents/100k (vs 381/100k nationally). Overall crime 61% lower than national average.
Demographics
  • Pop: ~22,000 residents. Approx half are white; second largest group is Asian (mainly Vietnamese).
  • Health: Excellent healthcare; relatively low air pollution compared to central LA.
3. Housing Conditions
  • Structure: Single-family houses on relatively large lots.
  • Tenure: 91% owner-occupied. High residential stability (2/3 lived there >10 years).
  • Environment: Streets lined with eucalyptus trees (legacy of windbreaks).
4. The Future
Northwood represents the opposite end of the economic spectrum to places like Jembatan Besi.
Irvine regularly features as a top-ranked city for safety and management. High-quality housing, transport, and climate ensure it retains its reputation and high property values even during downturns.
